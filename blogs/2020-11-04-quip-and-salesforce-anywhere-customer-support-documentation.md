We are excited to announce that we are migrating all of Quip and Salesforce Anywhere’s customer support articles and documentation to Salesforce’s Help and Training Portal ! Now, you'll be able to request Quip and Salesforce Anywhere technical support from the same place you get support for your other Salesforce products. Beginning on Monday, December 1, 2020, we will no longer accept new cases via quipsupport.com .
